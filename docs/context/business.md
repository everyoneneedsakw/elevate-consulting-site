# Business Context

## What Elevate Consulting does

Elevate Consulting (operating as "Elevate Consulting & Concierge" / "Everyone
Needs a Katie West") builds and implements operational systems for
service-based businesses — not just recommends them. The positioning is
explicitly hands-on: systems are built *with and for* the client's team, not
handed over as a slide deck.

## Core services

1. **Google Workspace Systems** — folder systems, SOP libraries, dashboards,
   CRM trackers
2. **SOPs & Playbooks** — documented processes a whole team can actually follow
3. **CRM & Automation** — lead tracking and follow-up systems
4. **Operations Consulting** — scaling, structure, and process improvement

## Core industries

Three named verticals, each with a dedicated page:

- **Roofing Companies** — operations, estimating, crew systems (brand accent:
  restrained deep emerald instead of the sitewide mauve)
- **Real Estate Professionals** — agent systems, CRM, transaction workflows
- **Service Businesses** — operations and scaling systems (general)

The site explicitly welcomes work "beyond" these three — they're the proven
core, not a hard boundary.

## Audience

Independent professionals, growing teams, and established companies in the
above industries who feel operationally overwhelmed — disorganized systems,
manual processes, tools that don't talk to each other — and want someone who
will both diagnose *and* build the fix, not just consult.

## Business goals (current)

- Primary conversion action: **book a discovery call** / **free audit**
  (wording for the free-audit offer is flagged for a future copy review —
  see `content-freeze.md`)
- Secondary: direct email contact for people who aren't sure where to start
- The site is pre-launch / early-stage polish — Services detail pages and the
  "Systems Shop" are intentionally "Coming Soon," not soft-launched with
  placeholder pricing

## What this is not

- Not a generic "SaaS consulting" shop — no stock-photo office imagery, no
  generic case-study cards, no vague "we help businesses grow" language
- Not productized/self-serve yet — every current CTA leads to a human
  conversation, not a purchase (that changes with the Systems Shop — see
  `roadmap.md`)
