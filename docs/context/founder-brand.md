# Founder & Brand

## Katie West

Founder of Elevate Consulting. The brand is explicitly founder-led — the About
page's entire argument is credibility through direct experience, not
credentials: *"I Don't Just Study Operations. I've Lived Inside Them."* (this
exact headline is content-frozen — see `content-freeze.md`).

Positioning pillars, from the approved About copy:

- Learns an unfamiliar business, tool, or process fast — no long ramp-up
- Talks to the people actually doing the work, not just leadership
- Simplifies before automating, instead of automating existing chaos
- Builds documentation and systems simple enough for the client's team to run
  without her
- Bridges the communication gap between leadership, employees, systems, and
  customers
- Texas-based, remote-capable; 10+ years experience, 50+ businesses served,
  3 core industries (see `business.md`)

## Voice

Direct, confident, warm, editorial — not corporate, not "girlboss," not
generic SaaS copywriting. Short declarative sentences mixed with longer
explanatory ones. Dashes for emphasis. First person ("I," "we" used somewhat
interchangeably for the founder/business voice). No emoji, no exclamation-mark
inflation, no buzzword stacking.

## Future collaborator model

Katie has indicated the business may expand beyond a solo-founder model to
include future collaborators. No specifics are locked in yet — when this
happens, the brand/voice guidance above should be revisited to decide what
stays founder-specific ("I've lived inside them") versus what generalizes to
a team voice ("we"). Flag this explicitly rather than assuming either
direction.

## Brand name usage

The site uses "Elevate Consulting" as the primary brand mark (homepage nav
currently renders it as "ELEVATE CONSULTING." all-caps; every other page uses
mixed-case "Elevate Consulting" — this is an existing, approved inconsistency
between the homepage and interior pages, not a bug to silently fix).
"Everyone Needs a Katie West" and "Elevate Consulting & Concierge" also appear
as the fuller business identity in places (e.g. email templates, project
naming) — treat these as valid alternate references to the same business, not
competing brands.
