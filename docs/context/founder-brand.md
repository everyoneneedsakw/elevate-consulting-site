# Founder & Brand

## Katie West

Founder of Elevate Consulting & Concierge. The brand is explicitly
founder-led — the About page's argument is credibility through direct,
hands-on experience and sharp strategic insight, not credentials alone.
Katie actively iterates on this positioning herself through Pages CMS (the
hero headline has already changed once this way, from *"I Don't Just Study
Operations. I've Lived Inside Them"* to *"THE VISIONARY. THE STRATEGIST. The
Problem-Solver"*) — don't treat either specific wording as permanently fixed;
check `content/pages/about.yml` for what's actually live. See
`content-freeze.md` for how the freeze applies to Claude versus to Katie's
own edits.

Positioning pillars, from the approved About copy (may be reworded by Katie
over time — the underlying pillars below are the durable part):

- Learns an unfamiliar business, tool, or process fast — no long ramp-up
- Talks to the people actually doing the work, not just leadership
- Simplifies before automating, instead of automating existing chaos
- Builds documentation and systems simple enough for the client's team to run
  without her
- Bridges the communication gap between leadership, employees, systems, and
  customers
- Texas-based, remote-capable; 10+ years experience, 50+ businesses served,
  3 core industries (see `business.md`)

## Voice

Direct, confident, warm, editorial — not corporate, not "girlboss," not
generic SaaS copywriting. Short declarative sentences mixed with longer
explanatory ones. Dashes for emphasis. First person ("I," "we" used somewhat
interchangeably for the founder/business voice). No emoji, no exclamation-mark
inflation, no buzzword stacking.

## Future collaborator model

Katie has indicated the business may expand beyond a solo-founder model to
include future collaborators. No specifics are locked in yet — when this
happens, the brand/voice guidance above should be revisited to decide what
stays founder-specific (first-person, "I") versus what generalizes to a team
voice ("we"). Flag this explicitly rather than assuming either direction.

## Brand name usage

Historically the site used "Elevate Consulting" as the primary brand mark
(homepage nav rendered "ELEVATE CONSULTING." all-caps; interior pages used
mixed-case "Elevate Consulting" — an approved, intentional inconsistency, not
a bug). Katie has since started editing the About page's own `site.brand_text`
field to "Elevate Consulting & Concierge" via Pages CMS — each page's brand
text is now an independently editable field
(`content/pages/<page>.yml` → `site.brand_text`), so it's expected and fine
for different pages to carry different brand-text states at different times
as Katie rolls a naming update out page by page, rather than needing a
single sitewide value to change atomically. "Everyone Needs a Katie West" is
also a valid alternate reference to the same business (e.g. email templates).
Don't "fix" an inconsistency between pages here without checking whether it's
mid-rollout.
